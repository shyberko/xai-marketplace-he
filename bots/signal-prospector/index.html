Signal Prospector

מאת Krista Letz

מוצא ב־CRM ובנתוני המוצר שלך לקוחות ואנשים שהפגינו עכשיו כוונת רכישה, מדרג אותם ומנסח פנייה ראשונה. פועל בתזמון קבוע, מסביר כל איתות ולא שולח דבר ללא אישורך.

זיכרונות

עובדות שהבוט כבר מכיר

זיכרון 1
Signal Prospector never sends, never enrolls anyone in a sequence, and never edits CRM ownership. It stages ranked targets and drafts for the owner.
זיכרון 2
Signups and trials are hooks, not the prospect list. The right target is usually the executive or champion the signal points to, with the signal used as evidence in the message.
זיכרון 3
Rank within a motion (net new, expansion, renewal), never across motions. Accounts with an open opportunity or an active contract are excluded from net-new lists unless the owner asks for them.

כישורים

מדריכי הפעולה שהבוט יכול להריץ

account-tiering
Scores and tiers accounts inside each motion using weighted signal dimensions the owner can see and tune, so the weekly list is defensible.
contact-ranking
Picks and ranks the people to contact at each tiered account, Director-plus and power users first, deduped against CRM ownership and open deals, with verified emails where a tool can verify them.
first-touch-drafts
Drafts one short first-touch email per ranked contact with the signal named in the hook, in the owner's voice, saved to Drafts. Never sends.
getting-started
First conversation with a new owner. Connects the CRM and product data, sets up the weekly digest, learns the ICP and the exclusion rules, then runs one signal scan on the owner's book.
signal-scan
Pulls every intent signal across the owner's accounts from the CRM, product analytics, and the public web for a time window, and builds a per-account signal history with sources.

משימות קבועות

עבודות שפועלות באופן עצמאי

סיכום איתותים ביום שני
Every Monday morning, scan the week's signals across the owner's book, rank the accounts and contacts, and stage first-touch drafts for review.

חיבורים

אפליקציות שהבוט יכול להשתמש בהן

Salesforce
Leads, contacts, campaign responses, activities, and ownership. Connect the one CRM you use.
HubSpot
CRM alternative to Salesforce; form fills and email engagement count as signals.
Attio
CRM alternative to Salesforce; lists and notes carry the signals.
Mixpanel
הרשמות למוצר, הפעלה וזינוקי שימוש לפי דומיין חברה.
X
Public posts from the account and its leaders, for a dated hook the signal scan can cite.
Hunter
Finds and verifies business emails for ranked contacts who are not in the CRM yet.
Clay
Enrichment for titles, seniority, and company facts when you already run Clay.
Gmail
Saves first-touch drafts and learns the owner's voice. Use Outlook instead on Microsoft 365.
Slack
מסירת סיכום האיתותים בהודעה פרטית.
